## About the Project

**The Other Side of the Story** explores the reality that two people can share the same relationship, conversation, or difficult moment and experience it in very different ways.

What one person sees may not be what the other person feels. What sounds like indifference may come from hurt. What feels like criticism to one person may be an attempt to explain something important to the other.

This book invites readers to slow down, listen more carefully, communicate honestly, recognize their own assumptions, and take responsibility for the part they bring into a relationship. It does not assume that one person is always right or that one person must always be wrong. It makes room for perspective, accountability, boundaries, and growth.

The website serves as the companion reading experience for the book, with chapter pages, background information, and reflection prompts.

## Design and Reading Experience

The website uses a consistent literary presentation across its pages, with responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

The visual palette gradually moves from darker, more restrained tones toward warmer and lighter colors as the reading journey progresses. The design is intended to reflect the movement from tension and uncertainty toward reflection, responsibility, and understanding.

Shared site elements include:

- Consistent masthead and navigation.
- Sticky primary navigation for easier movement through the site.
- A coordinated footer with links to the main sections and legal pages.
- Readable chapter widths, generous line spacing, and responsive typography.
- A book cover and supporting imagery where appropriate.
