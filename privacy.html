Privacy Policy

How this website handles information

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the website for The Other Side of the Story by Bradley Hobbs is intended to handle information when you visit. The site is provided primarily to introduce the book, share reading material and reflections, and provide a way to contact the author.

Information You Choose to Provide

This website does not require you to create an account or submit personal information to read its pages. If you contact the author by email, the information you include in your message, including your email address and any details you choose to share, will be received through the email service you use and the author's email provider.

Email Correspondence

You may contact Bradley Hobbs at brad.hobbs13@icloud.com. Please avoid sending sensitive personal, financial, medical, or confidential information unless it is necessary and you are comfortable sharing it by email. Email communications may be retained as needed to respond and maintain correspondence.

Cookies, Analytics, and Hosting

This website is published using GitHub Pages. GitHub and the systems that deliver the website may process technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device information, and request logs, as part of providing, maintaining, and securing the hosting service. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies. This site does not intentionally use its own advertising cookies or collect information through a visitor account system.

External Links

This website may link to other websites or services. Those sites operate independently and have their own privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information or using their services.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a communication to the author, please make contact so the matter can be addressed.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the website or its practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published.

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to brad.hobbs13@icloud.com.

This page provides general website privacy information and is not legal advice.
